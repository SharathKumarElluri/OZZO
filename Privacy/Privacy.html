OUR COMMITMENT

Privacy, Without The Confusion.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected when you use OZZO websites, products and services, and how that information may be used.

01

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly when you create an account, place an order, contact our support team, subscribe to communications or interact with our website.

This may include information such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery details, order details and information you choose to provide to us.

02

How We Use Information

Information may be used to process orders, provide customer support, communicate with you, improve our website and services, and provide a smoother shopping experience.

We may also use information to help maintain website security, prevent misuse and understand how our services are being used.

03

Information Sharing

OZZO does not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers and partners when necessary to operate our services, process payments, deliver orders or provide customer support.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect our rights, users or services.

04

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences, improve functionality, understand usage and support relevant website experiences.

Browser settings may provide controls for managing or disabling cookies.

05

Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss or alteration.

No online service can guarantee complete security, so we encourage you to use strong passwords and protect your account credentials.

06

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion or restriction of certain personal information.

You can also choose whether to receive certain marketing communications by using available unsubscribe options.
